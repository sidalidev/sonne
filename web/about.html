Our principles

  • Your data belongs to you.
  • No account. No tracking. No ads.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you choose it.
  • The code will be published, so anyone can check this.

What leaves your browser

Ring commands

To: the relay that served this page. Encrypted: yes, end to end. Every command is a message of the same size: the relay cannot tell a ring from a stop or a status check. Nobody without your phone's key can send or read one. A phone still on Sonne 1.5 or older gets the old format, signed only, until it updates.

Phone status

From your phone, through the relay. Encrypted: yes, end to end. Only this browser and your other paired devices can read the battery, ringing state and phone model.

What the relay sees

A random chain id, message sizes (the same for every command), times, and your IP address while connected. It never sees the content. It writes nothing to disk: waiting commands are dropped after 15 minutes, the last seen time after 24 hours.

On sonne.sidali.dev, the web server in front of the relay logs each request (IP address, time, path, never the chain id) for 7 days.

Stored in this browser only

Non-exportable keys for your phones, their names and the activity list. No cookies, no analytics. Remove a phone from My phones to delete it here.
